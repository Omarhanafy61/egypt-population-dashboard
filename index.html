Findings & limitations

Use the governorate and year filters to compare population size, simulated growth, demographic mix and density. The age chart estimates the share of children (0–14), working-age adults (15–64), and seniors (65+).

Data transparency: All figures are illustrative synthetic values created for a classroom demonstration. They are not official Egyptian population estimates, birth/death statistics, or census measurements. Replace with verified CAPMAS or other official source data before making factual claims.
